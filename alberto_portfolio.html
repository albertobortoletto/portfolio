  • Designed and built an intelligent wristband that predicts panic attacks ~60 seconds before onset by monitoring PPG, EDA, skin temperature, IMU, and respiration rate.
  • Trained a 2-layer LSTM classifier (Normal / Onset / Panic / Recovery) on synthetically generated multi-profile physiological data; quantised to INT8 via TFLite Micro for cloud-free edge inference on an ESP32-S3.
  • Engineered an adaptive haptic feedback loop (3× LRA motors, DRV2605L drivers) that re-evaluates the user's physiological response every 40 seconds and shifts strategy across four phases to prevent neural adaptation.
  • Won 1st place at the Elevator Innovation Hub Hackathon 2026.
